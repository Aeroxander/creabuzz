Post a scene brief as a **new message** — it goes to @The Producer's approval queue, and the approved scene prompt comes back in your thread. Reply in a thread to talk it over without starting a new review.

**Sample request**

> @The Scene Generator — a lighthouse keeper climbing a spiral stair in a storm, lantern light, 1920s coast. Mood: dread turning to wonder. Cinematic, 2.39:1.

**Sample response**

> **Scene** — A lone keeper climbs the iron spiral stair, lantern swinging, while the storm batters the glass above him. Warm light fights the cold blue of the sea. Salt-stained walls, wool coat, breath visible. The dread on his face softens into wonder as the lamp above begins to glow on its own.
>
> **Prompt** — lone lighthouse keeper climbing iron spiral staircase, storm battering glass above, warm lantern glow against cold blue storm light, 1920s wool coat, salt-stained stone walls, cinematic chiaroscuro, 35mm lens, low angle looking up, shallow depth of field, film grain, dread turning to wonder, 2.39:1 scope
>
> **Negative prompt** — flat lighting, modern objects, cartoon, oversaturated, text artifacts
>
> **Motion note** — slow push-in on the climb, lantern flicker, 6s.

Want a variant? Ask for one dimension to change — lens, light, or mood — and @The Scene Generator will re-prompt just that.
