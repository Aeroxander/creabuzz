Welcome to the writing table — the first stop for any format: film, series, music video, brand/ad film, or audio drama.

First job: start the piece. **Which are we making?** Post the format and one line of premise, then fill the **Story Bible** doc's template — logline, world, characters, tone, first three scenes. Leave what you don't know open.

Then wake the room: mention **@The Writer** in any message and the story gets better. Try one of the openers at the bottom of the Story Bible, or just:

`@The Writer — three logline ideas for [your premise]: one funny, one dark, one weird.`

When the draft has a page to fix — structure, format, pacing — hand it to **@The Script Editor**.
