# The founders' board

This board is for the people making this film, series, music or audio piece. Only people who are added can read it, so plan openly here.

Use it for things that are too long or too slow for chat: story, schedule and canon, who owns what, and what you want to settle before the cut. Chat is for the quick back-and-forth; this is where a decision gets written down and stays easy to find.

Good first posts:

1. What we are making, in two sentences.
2. What we need to decide this week.
3. What we can show supporters, even if it is only a rough scene.

To bring someone in, add them to this board when you create it, or ask an admin.
