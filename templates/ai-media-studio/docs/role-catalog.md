# Role Catalog

Who makes an AI film? The full production role map — what each role owns, and whether this studio ships it today or leaves it open for you to hire.

**Ships** = a studio collaborator you can mention right now. **Open** = a real role on a real production that this template doesn't staff yet; the human team covers it, or you hire for it.

## Development

| Role | What they do | Ships here? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Showrunner** | Owns the story and the schedule at once — the person every call reports up to | Open — the human in the chair; @The Producer runs the board for them |
| **Story Writer** | Premise, logline, beats, scenes, dialogue — the first draft of everything | Ships as **@The Writer** |
| **Script Editor** | Scene structure, page format, pacing, and a dialog-driven dialogue polish pass | Ships as **@The Script Editor** |
| **Lore-Continuity** | Keeps the Story Bible true and every asset agreeing with it; flags contradictions | Ships as **@The Continuity Keeper** |
| **Researcher** | Facts, period detail, reference hunting so the bible isn't built on vibes | Open |

## Visual

| Role | What they do | Ships here? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Visual Style Director** | Locks the look — palette, stock, lens language — and keeps every shot inside it | Open (the Story Bible's tone & palette section is where they'd start) |
| **Storyboard** | Thumbnails the sequence: what's in frame, in what order, cut how | Open |
| **Character Designer** | Faces, wardrobe, silhouette — the wording the whole team repeats verbatim | Open (the bible's Look fields seed it) |
| **Prompt-Scene Generator** | Scene brief in, vivid description and ready-to-paste image/video prompt out | Ships as **@The Scene Generator** |
| **Generation Ops** | Runs the queue: seeds, batches, retries, versions, and which render is canon | Open — the prompt is the deliverable until a render service is connected |

## Production

| Role | What they do | Ships here? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Producer** | Breakdowns, schedule reality, decision logs, digests — and the approval queue | Ships as **@The Producer** |
| **Voice-Casting** | Matches voices to characters: range, age, texture, and who can actually read the part | Open |
| **Sound & Music** | Score, sound design, foley descriptors, and audio-drama voice scripts and cues | Ships as **@The Sound & Music** |
| **Editor** | The assembly and the cut — pacing decided in the timeline, not on the page | Open |

## Post

| Role | What they do | Ships here? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VFX-Compositing** | Layers the shot: cleanup, comp, screen inserts, invisible effects | Open |
| **Color-Grade** | The final look pass — continuity of light and color across every shot | Open |
| **QA-Critic** | Watch-through with a checklist: continuity breaks, audio pops, format slips, bible drift | Open (partially covered — @The Continuity Keeper flags canon drift) |

## Ship

| Role | What they do | Ships here? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Marketing-Trailer** | Trailer, key art copy, the thirty-second cut that sells the piece | Open (briefs land in #scene-gen like any other) |
| **Distribution-Community** | Release plan, platform cutdowns, and the audience conversation after launch | Open |
| **Legal-Clearances** | Music rights, likeness, trademarks, locations — the "can we ship this" check | Open |

## How to staff the open roles

Six roles ship with the studio; the rest are honest gaps, not oversights — a small team covers them with a human wearing the hat, and the work still flows through the same four channels.

When the Project Board lands, post open roles there for equity and hire the gap instead of stretching the crew.
