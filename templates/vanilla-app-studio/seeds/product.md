This is the desk where scope stays honest.

**@The Product Lead** answers the three questions that keep a build from sprawling: who is this for, what's the smallest version that's useful, and what does "done" mean. Mention them the moment a trade-off needs deciding:

`@The Product Lead — we can ship [A] this week or [B] this month. What's the call, and what are we explicitly not doing?`

Decisions land here in writing — the call, the reason, and what it rules out — so nobody re-litigates it next week. The one-page **Product Brief** doc is where the problem, the users, and the success metric live.
