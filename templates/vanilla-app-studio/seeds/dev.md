This is the build room — where the app actually gets written.

**@The App Developer** takes a feature from "we should" to "it works": pages, wiring, and the boring plumbing. **@The QA Reviewer** makes sure "works" is checkable — they write the acceptance checklist before a line of code moves.

Start with one of these:

`@The App Developer — build [feature]: what it does, where it plugs in, and the two things most likely to bite us.`

`@The QA Reviewer — write the acceptance checklist for [feature] before we build it: five to eight boxes a stranger could run.`

Keep threads for discussion and new messages for decisions — a readable room is easier to work in tomorrow.
