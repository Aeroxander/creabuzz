# Privacy note

Last updated: [date]

[Operator name] runs this Creaton server. This note says what we keep, why, and what you can ask us to do.

## What is public

Everything you post in public places is public by design: your profile (name, picture, description), posts, replies,
votes, boards you start and join, and who you follow. It is signed with your key and can be copied by other servers and
apps that use the same open protocol (Nostr). Anything you do on a blockchain (a wallet address, a vote, a claim, Shares
you hold) is public and permanent; nobody, including us, can delete it.

## What we keep on our server

- The public records above, so we can show them.
- **Messages** (in the main menu): end-to-end encrypted with your key. We store them but cannot read what they say; we
  can see whose accounts they are between and when they were sent.
- **Everything inside a community**, its private rooms and boards and its direct messages included: these are **not**
  end-to-end encrypted. They are protected on the way to us and stored on our server [on encrypted disks], where the
  people who run it can read them. We read them only to keep the service running, to handle a report, or when the law
  requires it. When someone reports a message, the community's moderators who handle the report see it.
- Technical logs: IP addresses, times and errors, kept for [30] days to keep the service secure and working.
- Reports you or others send to moderators, and moderators' actions, for as long as needed to handle them.
- Backups of all of the above, so the service can be restored after a failure, kept for [14] days in [where they are
  kept]. Something deleted here stays in the backups until they expire.
- [If error reporting is switched on:] error reports from the app, which contain the page and the error, not your key.

We do not sell your data, show ads or use advertising trackers.

## What stays on your device

Your key (unless you use a passkey or a wallet, which keep it themselves), your settings and drafts are stored in your
browser. Clearing your browser data removes them; save your recovery key first.

## Other services

Some steps go through other companies, which see what that step needs under their own privacy policies. We pass them
only what each step needs.

- **The chain node**, [name of the page's chain node provider]: the app in your browser asks it for public blockchain
  records, so it sees your IP address and which accounts and projects you look at. Our server asks [name of the
  server's chain node provider] to check a passkey account when you link it to your name.
- **ZeroDev**, for passkey accounts: each on-chain step you take from a passkey account goes through ZeroDev's bundler,
  and its paymaster covers the fee. ZeroDev sees your account's address, the step and your IP address.
- **Your passkey** is kept by your device or password manager (for example Apple, Google or Microsoft) under their own
  terms. We never see it. If you use a browser wallet instead, its maker's terms apply to it.
- **Pictures and links hosted elsewhere.** A picture someone posts from another site (in a profile or a post) loads
  straight from that site when you view it, so that site sees your IP address and when you looked. A link takes you to
  its site.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR) you can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it,
delete it or object to how we use it. We can delete what is on our server; we cannot delete copies on other servers or
anything on a blockchain. Write to [email]. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Changes

If this note changes, we post the change in General and the date above changes.
