# Creaton as the first example project

A fresh visitor to the server sees an empty Discover page: "No projects on this server yet." The cheapest fix is a real
first project that is Creaton itself, published from your own account through the normal **Pitch a project** form, so
people see what a pitch looks like, what a progress update looks like and where backers talk. Nothing here needs a script
or a key in the repository: you type it in as the operator, edit anything you disagree with, and it is yours.

Everything below is a draft for you to change. The role shares are placeholders: they are a decision about who owns what, and
only you can make it. Keep the test-version wording; the app already says "Testnet" beside the logo, and the pitch should not
read as an offer of returns.

## 1. The pitch (Discover → Pitch a project)

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| Project id | `creaton` |
| Name | Creaton |
| One-line pitch (200 characters at most) | A place where a creative or technical team pitches an idea, raises money from people who believe in it, and answers to them with public progress. |

**Description**

> Creaton is where projects get started, funded and held to what they said.
>
> **How it works.** Share an idea in a sentence and see who would back it. When there is real interest, open a sale. The
> people who back it become owners and vote on how the money is used. Work is paid when reviewers approve it, and a public
> track record shows what happened, including what did not go to plan.
>
> **Where we are.** This is a test version. It runs on a test network with pretend money, and the contracts have not been
> audited. We are inviting a small group to try it, tell us what is confusing, and start projects of their own.
>
> **What we are making next.** A calmer first hour for newcomers, clearer decisions for the people who back a project, and
> better tools for the room owners who keep a community healthy.
>
> **Who we are looking for.** A designer who likes making hard things feel simple, an engineer who enjoys small careful
> changes, and a community lead who wants to help the first projects succeed.

**Roles** (the first one is yours; shares are placeholders)

| Role | Share |
|---|---|
| Founder | 40% |
| Designer | 10% |
| Engineer | 15% |
| Community lead | 8% |

Choose "Founder" as the role you take.

## 2. A first progress update (Home → post, or the project's page)

> Creaton is open to a first group of invited people. This week: Home and the social feed became one page, new
> communities start from two plain choices (a creative or a technical project), and room owners can remove a message.
> Next: a calmer first hour, and your projects. If something confuses you, say so here. That is the most useful thing you can
> tell us.

Mark it as an update for the project if the post box offers "Launch update" once the project has a launch of its own.

## 3. Two first posts for the supporters board

1. **What would you want to see before you back a project?** "A clear one-line pitch, who is on the team, what the first
   milestone is and how you would know it was met. What else?"
2. **Pitch your own.** "Have an idea? Share it in a sentence on the Start page. We will help you shape it into something
   people can back."

## What this gives a first visitor

- Discover shows a project instead of an empty box, and the pitch page shows the roles and the plan.
- Home's Projects tab has a real update in it, so "following a project" can be tried straight away.
- The supporters board is not empty, so the first person to write there is not the first person to write.

## Not done, and why

- It is a pitch, not a raise: opening a sale needs the launch kit on the network you choose, and the "unaudited" notice applies.
  A raise for Creaton itself is a separate decision and is not drafted here.
- Nothing is published for you. If you would rather have a script that publishes these through the command line with a key
  you supply, say so; it is about an hour, and it would live next to the other seed scripts, not in the app.
