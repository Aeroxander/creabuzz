# Creaton as the first contributor project (seed content for the pilot)

A first visitor to the server should find a real project with work they can do today, not an empty Discover page. The
first project is Creaton itself, started as a **contributor project**: no token sale, Shares earned by reviewed work,
with a few small pieces of work open to anyone, including people who know nothing about crypto. It is also the pilot's
first project: the useful work people do here is the evidence the pilot is looking for
([STATUS.md](STATUS.md)). It matches the contributor run in [PILOT-RUNBOOK.md](PILOT-RUNBOOK.md) (updated 2026-10-31
for D139, D144 and D145: reviewers by name, who decides a split, accepting for review and finishing the review).

Everything below is a draft for you to type in through the normal pages and change as you like. Nothing here needs a
script or a key in the repository. The numbers are the Start form's defaults, and the founder decides them. Keep the
test-version wording: the app says "Testnet" beside the logo, Shares on a test network are test records, and nothing
here offers a return.

**Before you start:** the network the server is built for needs the contributor kit (on Sepolia, after
`scripts/sepolia-deploy.sh --broadcast`). Pick **three reviewers** (or two, see below) who are not you and not the people
likely to do the work, and ask them first: who they are, how fast they will answer, any tie to you. Each of them signs up
and uses **Show your name with this account** (the account strip on the contributor screens) before you start, so you can
add them from the list by name. The founder cannot be a reviewer. Every piece of work below is posted on
an open board called **supporters**: a community with no boards yet is offered it (with a private founders board) on
its Boards page; otherwise start one with that name, open to everyone.

## 1. The project (Start → Start a contributor project)

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| Project name | Creaton |
| What it is | A place to meet, discuss projects, do useful work and earn a say in what a project does. |
| Short name | CREA |
| Your starting Shares | 100,000 |
| Shares available for work | 300,000 |
| Most for one work area | 25,000 |
| Reviewers | add the three from the list, by name (each shows as a name, not "Not linked to anyone here") |
| How many reviewers must agree | leave empty: two of the three decide |

**With only two reviewers**, press **Let any one decide** instead (it sets "How many reviewers must agree" to 1): the first
answer decides, a turn-down as much as an approval, and the other reviewer cannot overrule it. Leaving it empty with two
reviewers needs both to agree, and a split then decides nothing until the piece lapses (D145). Tell the reviewers which
rule you chose.

Before you press **Start the project**, read the preview with the people you invite in mind: at the start you hold all
100,000 Shares, so you decide every vote; the 300,000 for work do not vote until they are awarded.

**First work areas** (press **Add another work area** for each). Each check is what finished work must meet. The app
publishes these words with the project, so contributors read them under Ways to help and the submit form fills them in.
Keep them exactly as you want them judged: they cannot be changed once the project exists.

| Work area | Shares | What finished work looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Your first hour | 10,000 | A written account of your first hour on Creaton (signing up, finding this project, submitting this work), naming each place you got stuck or unsure, posted on the supporters board. |
| Phone check | 15,000 | Screenshots of Start, Work and Contributions on a phone, each with what is hard to read or tap and a suggested change, posted on the supporters board. |

Two work areas, as the runbook's step 9 asks: both need no technical skill, and a stranger can check each against its
words. That sets aside 25,000 of the 300,000: the rest is room for suggested work and later work areas, each approved by a
vote. Two that fit well later, as suggestions: **A plain guide** (a one-page guide in plain words to how work becomes
Shares here, that the reviewers find accurate) and **Bug reports** (a bug with the steps to reproduce it, reproduced by
a reviewer).

## 2. The invitation (Home → post to General)

> **Help build Creaton.** Two pieces of work are open on the Creaton project, and you need no Shares and no crypto
> experience to do them: notes on your first hour here, or a check of three screens on your phone.
>
> Open the project and look under **Ways to help**: each piece says what finished work looks like. Pick one and press
> **Submit work for this**.
>
> What happens next: I accept your piece for review (it shows under **Needs you**), and the reviewers check it against
> the work area's words; we aim to answer within three days. Once they approve, you or I press **Finish the review**,
> which starts a 7-day window in which anyone can object. Then you mark it finished, and the project votes on an award,
> which takes about two and a half days. If the reviewers turn it down, you will see why, and you can fix it and submit
> again. Approval alone does not guarantee Shares: the award is its own vote, and right now I hold every Share that
> votes. The whole run is about fifteen days, and most of the second week is a wait.
>
> Shares here are the project's voting tokens on a test network: test records that will not move to a real network.
> Nothing is for sale and nothing is paid out.
>
> Have a better idea? Suggest it under **Suggested work** on the Work tab, with what you would do and the Shares you ask
> for. A holder can put it to a vote.

The three-day answer is a promise you make, not a rule the app enforces: a submission nobody answers lapses after 30
days. If you cannot keep it, say so in the post.

## 3. Two first posts for the supporters board

1. **Where did you get stuck?** "If anything about Creaton confused you, reply here, even with one sentence. If you want
   Shares for it, the “Your first hour” work area is for exactly this."
2. **What should we work on next?** "Suggest a piece of work on the Creaton project's Work tab, under Suggested work: what

   work area anyone can submit to."

## 4. A first update (the project's page → Post update)

> Creaton is open to a first small group. Two pieces of work are open under Ways to help, and anyone can suggest more.
> We are watching where people get stuck, how long reviews take, and whether the award votes make sense, so tell us when
> something is unclear. That is the most useful thing you can tell us.

## What a first visitor gets

- Discover shows a project, and its Overview says how many work areas are open and that no Shares are needed to submit,
  with **Join the discussion** beside it.
- Ways to help lists two concrete pieces of work with their exact words; neither needs technical skill.
- The header says what is true and checked: no token sale at setup, new Shares only from awards, Shares locked.
- The supporters board and General are not empty, so the first person to write is not the first person there.

## For the pilot

Invite, on their own devices and accounts and with no walkthrough: the reviewers, a few contributors (at least one
new to crypto and one doing non-technical work), and someone who joins a few days late and knows nobody. Each gets their
own invite link (the runbook's step 5). Watch for how
people find the work, the first submission, how long review takes, a rejection or a disagreement, the 7-day window, the
award vote, and the visit after a day away. Ask afterwards: what could you do without Shares, who decides right now, and
what does approval give you? The day-by-day plan and what to watch are in [PILOT-RUNBOOK.md](PILOT-RUNBOOK.md); what to
write down is in [PILOT-REPORT.md](PILOT-REPORT.md).

## Not done, and why

- **No raise.** The release order is the social MVP, then contributor projects, then an audited raise. A raise for
  Creaton itself would be a separate decision on that later path, after the audit and a legal read.
- **Started from Start, not from the Projects board.** A pitch on the Projects board can also start a contributor
  project (each role becomes a work area), but its words are generic ("the work agreed for this role is delivered"). For
  the pilot, concrete work areas like those above are easier to judge and to explain.
- **Nothing is published for you.** If you would rather have a script that publishes the posts with a key you supply,
  say so; it would live beside the other seed scripts, not in the app.
