# Direction: a project keeps its promises on the record

Status: the owner's direction, written 2026-11-10. It replaces the slice order in
[SOCIAL-FIRST-PROPOSAL.md](SOCIAL-FIRST-PROPOSAL.md) and [COLD-START-PROPOSAL.md](COLD-START-PROPOSAL.md) where they differ.
Nothing here changes a contract or an economic number.

## The one sentence

Creaton is where a project states a goal, does its work in the open, and lets anyone see whether it kept its promises.

## Who it is for

- **A founder** with a real side project who wants help and an audience, and is willing to be held to a goal.
- **A helper** (a person, or later an agent) who wants a clear task, a fair check, and a record of work that was accepted.
- **A supporter** who wants to tell a project that delivers from one that only talks.

## The four things every project has

1. **A goal.** One primary goal in the founder's words, and how we will know it was reached. Shorter goals sit under it
   (this quarter, this month, this week).
2. **A weekly update.** What happened, what is next, what help is needed. It reports against the goals.
3. **Tasks with a finish line.** Each task says what "done" means before anyone starts. Finished work comes with evidence
   (a link, a file, a result), and someone other than the person who did it checks it.
4. **A record.** Promised, delivered, missed, changed course. Misses and reversals stay visible. A forecast is written
   down before a move and marked afterwards as held or not held.

Shares are how a project thanks people whose work was accepted. They follow the record; they are not the point of it.

## Ready for agents from the start

We do not build agents first. We build so an agent can join later without a redesign:

- **A helper is an account, not a person.** Tasks, offers, evidence and checks never assume a human. An agent account is
  labelled as an agent and names who runs it.
- **Tasks are readable by a machine.** A task carries its goal, its "done" words, its limits and its size as fields, not
  only as prose, so an agent can pick it up through the command line tool the server already has.
- **Evidence is recorded, not claimed.** What was submitted (links, files with their fingerprints, results) is stored with
  the task, so a check never rests on a helper's own summary.
- **Nobody grades their own work.** The same rule for people and agents: the one who did the task never checks it.
- **Limited powers.** An agent may offer, do work, submit evidence and draft text. It may not vote, award Shares, move
  money or accept its own work. Anything gated needs a person's yes.
- **Each project's knowledge stays with that project.**

## What changes on screen

| Page | Becomes |
|---|---|
| Project | The goal, this week's update, open help requests, and the record. Other tabs appear only when the project uses them. |
| Start | "What is your goal, and how will we know?" Everything else has a default. |
| Work | Tasks with a finish line, evidence and an independent check. |
| Home | A feed of project records: updates, kept and missed promises, help wanted. Price posts live here and nowhere else. |
| Portfolio | The projects you back or help, and how their promises are holding up. |
| Profile | A track record: work accepted, promises kept, for people and for agents. |

Hidden until a project needs them: the court, forecasts with money, trading, Explore, boards as a separate place.

## The first step, and the test

1. Rebuild the project page and Start around the goal and the record. The weekly update and help requests being built
   now are the first half of this.
2. Put three to five real founders on it, onboarded by hand.
3. Watch one number: **do they post a second and a third weekly update without being asked?** If yes, continue. If no,
   stop and rethink before building more.

## What could make this wrong

- Founders may not want their misses public.
- Supporters may not care about a record while no money is at stake.
- Showing returns, even only in the feed, is for counsel to read before it ships.

## Not now

Leads and delegation levels, Thanks rounds, ladder stages, the vouch, new contracts. Backups and notifications outside
the app are not optional, but they are operations, not direction.
