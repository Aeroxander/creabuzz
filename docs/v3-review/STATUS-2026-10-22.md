([SOCIAL-MVP-ROADMAP](SOCIAL-MVP-ROADMAP-2026-10-19.md)). Details of each decision are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md);
how to check each one by hand is in [TEST-STEPS.md](TEST-STEPS.md).

the mocked browser suite, and real-chain journeys on a throwaway local chain (and a Sepolia fork where said). None of it
is an audit or a hosted multi-person run.

## Only a hosted pilot can show

From the outside review (2026-10-22): one or two real projects, a founder, independent reviewers and several
contributors (one new to crypto, one doing non-technical work), on their own devices, with no walkthrough.

- Discovery, the first submission, how long review takes, a rejection or a disagreement, finishing, the award vote, and
  coming back a day later.
- Whether people can say who decides, what approval does and does not give, and what Shares are.
- Account and device recovery, a reviewer who is away, Needs you after a real pause.

Success is a useful piece of work that gets through review and an award vote with an outcome people understand.

- **Broadcast to Sepolia:** `scripts/sepolia-deploy.sh --broadcast`, with your own key in `.env`.
- **Host the server** with `deploy/compose` and its Creaton setup (D107: the image, read state, terms at sign-up,
  backups): a machine, a domain, the filled-in `.env`, and one practised restore.

- **The first project:** [CREATON-EXAMPLE-PROJECT.md](CREATON-EXAMPLE-PROJECT.md) is now contributor-first seed content

## Known gaps, not yet designed

- **A record reader independent of community membership** ([VISION_MODERATION.md](../../VISION_MODERATION.md)): a holder
  banned from a community must still be able to read the evidence needed to object, vote and leave. It needs its own
  design, with explicit evidence visibility, not open access to private chats. Proposed in
  [BOARD-MODERATION-DESIGN-2026-10-22.md](BOARD-MODERATION-DESIGN-2026-10-22.md) §5, as the prerequisite for board
  moderators elected by a project's holders.
- **Recovery on a hosted server** (passkeys and devices, wallet binding, backups) has not been run end to end. Live
  permission changes (a timeout, a ban, a removal, a board mute) have, on a local server (D96, D97).
- **Words in browser storage:** a work area's words that failed to publish are kept only in the browser that opened the
  vote (bounded, never evicted); the suggestion or filed evidence also carry them.

## Deferred on purpose

- **The project helper** (an AI assistant on a project): only if the pilot shows repeated questions that Ways to help and
  Needs you do not answer.
- **Fundraising explanations, and the raise itself:** on their own path, after the audit, the frozen scope, a verified
  deployment and a legal read ([AUDIT-SCOPE.md](AUDIT-SCOPE.md), [PILOT.md](PILOT.md)). A no-sale mainnet deployment still
  uses value-bearing governance contracts and needs the audit too.
- **Board moderation, later steps** ([BOARD-MODERATION-DESIGN-2026-10-22.md](BOARD-MODERATION-DESIGN-2026-10-22.md)):
  the record reader (step 3), then moderators chosen and overturned by a project's DAO (step 4) only if the pilot shows
  appointed moderators are not enough. No token rewards for engagement.
